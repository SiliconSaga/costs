# Layer C — Explainers and links

The cheapest layer and the first one built. One "where else to look" page, a handful of plain-language explainers each written once and dated, and the methods page that the tables will lean on.

## Where else to look

The [Sources and projects](../reference/sources.md) reference page is the public face of this layer: every existing publisher, tool, and dataset a resident or an analyst might use instead of, or alongside, this site, with what each covers and what it misses. The links are not the product; the gap they leave is the reason this site exists.

## Explainers

Each is one page, plain words, dated, and linked from the tables where the term appears.

- How to read an explanation of benefits.
- What a facility fee is, and why the same MRI costs several times more inside a hospital.
- What "in network," "allowed amount," and "negotiated rate" mean.
- How to ask for the cash price, and when it beats using insurance.
- Where to find the Medicare price for a procedure.
- What the 2021 and 2026 hospital rules require, and how to report a hospital that is not complying (the CMS complaint form).
- Where New Jersey's own efforts stand: the A5540 / S4501 claims-database bills, S3012 / A1729 on enforcement and debt collection, S3409 on facility fees. Statuses as found are on [Sources and projects](../reference/sources.md#pending-bills-202627-session); re-check before the explainer is published, because bills move.

## Methods

A page the tables link to for everything the one-sentence explanation leaves out: what the hospital files are and what they omit (physician fees billed separately, facility fees at off-campus sites, anesthesia, pathology, and the freestanding centers until Layer B), how the Medicare benchmark is computed and why it is approximate, how payer names are normalised, and how "N/A" and placeholder values are treated.
