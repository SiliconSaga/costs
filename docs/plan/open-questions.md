# Open questions

Questions no research item answers; they need a decision by the keeper, and some need the project to exist for a while first.

- **Whether the civic repositories move** from SiliconSaga to a Front State GitHub organization, and whether Front State becomes a nonprofit.
- **Submissions from outside Essex** from the start? Proposal: accept, publish once that county has its own facility list.
- **Individual rows or aggregates only** once a group clears the suppression threshold. Default: aggregates.
- **Facility-first or procedure-first** navigation, for an audience that mostly has one insurer through work and can choose only the facility.
- **Where the yearly county table lives**: here, on the ledger, or both. Proposal: generated here, cited there.
- **How far Ting stretches** to hold the bill codes and records before a sibling service is cleaner. Lean: stretch it.
- **When the homelab box is bought**, and whether Layer B waits for it or starts on a smaller box with one carrier. The shape is on [Homelab box](../architecture/homelab.md).
- **Whether raw insurer files are kept at all** after extraction, or only their hashes and the Parquet; this decides most of the box's disk.
- **The price site's generator**, decided at M1.
